DOPE MEDIA — BRAND & MARKETING GROWTH CHECKLIST

1. BRAND CLARITY
[ ] Can a new visitor understand what you offer in five seconds?
[ ] Is your ideal customer clearly defined?
[ ] Does your visual identity look consistent everywhere?
[ ] Is your main call to action obvious?

2. CONTENT SYSTEM
[ ] Do you have three to five repeatable content pillars?
[ ] Is there a realistic weekly publishing schedule?
[ ] Can one long-form idea become multiple short posts?
[ ] Does every post educate, build trust, or invite action?

3. WEBSITE & CONVERSION
[ ] Is the website fast and easy to use on mobile?
[ ] Does every important page have one primary action?
[ ] Are testimonials, work samples, and trust signals visible?
[ ] Are enquiries captured with a short, simple form?

4. LEAD FOLLOW-UP
[ ] Does every new enquiry receive a quick response?
[ ] Are leads tracked in one place?
[ ] Is there a clear next step after someone contacts you?
[ ] Do you follow up more than once?

5. SMART AUTOMATION
[ ] Which repeated weekly task takes the most time?
[ ] Can enquiries be routed automatically?
[ ] Can content drafts or reports be prepared with AI?
[ ] Is a human still reviewing customer-facing output?

6. MEASUREMENT
[ ] Is website analytics installed?
[ ] Do you know which channel creates qualified enquiries?
[ ] Are you tracking conversions instead of only likes?
[ ] Do you review results at least once each month?

NEXT STEP
Circle the three unchecked items most likely to improve revenue or save time.
Focus on those first, then review this checklist again in 30 days.
